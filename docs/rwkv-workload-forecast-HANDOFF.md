# RWKV workload forecast graph

The Statistics graph delegates simulation to the Anki fork's `aqt.rwkv_scheduler` forecast API. It passes the active search, forecast horizon, and number of simulated futures; polls the background job; and can cancel it. A compatible, running RWKV backend is required.

The graph plots daily review due and done, introductions, lapses, or study time. It defaults to 7 days and 3 paths; users can choose longer horizons or more paths. For Instant on a compatible fork with enough review history, three paths show the paired `R − σ`, `R`, and `R + σ` scenarios returned as `scenario_daily`. The central line and total use the actual R path, even if it is not between the two shifted scenarios. The tooltip identifies each scenario; the shaded area between shifted paths is illustrative and is not a confidence interval. The fork estimates σ from past daily prediction residuals. If there is insufficient history, or an older fork without scenarios, the three paths are ordinary sampled futures: the graph shows their mean and a rough mean ± sample standard deviation clipped to the simulated range. It recovers those values from the fork's type-7 linearly interpolated p1, p50, and p99. With 20 or more futures, lines show the mean and median and bands show the 5th–95th and 1st–99th percentiles. Changing metrics redraws the SVG, including its axis.

For an Instant forecast, the fork samples real historical study timings and successful-answer patterns, predicts recall with the Instant model, and applies the configured review limits, new-card limit toggle, queue refresh settings, and learning steps. Frequent full queue refreshes on a large search can take a long time. The forecast does not model skipped days, reviews outside the search, sibling burying, load balancing, or future settings changes. A search that includes RWKV-Curve scheduled cards is not supported by the Instant path. The complete algorithm and its limitations are documented in the fork's `docs/rwkv.MD` under “Workload Forecast”.

Newer fork builds report Instant results progressively per path and day. While the job runs, the graph draws the central R days that are ready and adds each shifted path and the spread when the corresponding values arrive; missing values are not interpolated. For stochastic forecasts it draws the provisional mean without percentile bands until the job completes. The progress count is in simulated path-days when this stream is available; old forks and Curve jobs retain path-level progress and a final graph. Cancelling leaves any displayed partial graph marked preliminary, and starting a new run clears the previous result.

The bridge is in `workload_forecast.py`, the data transformations are in `src/ts/workloadForecastData.ts`, and the graph and controls are in `src/ts/WorkloadForecastGraph.ts` and `src/ts/WorkloadForecast.svelte`. Verify the TypeScript tests and production build, package with `./package.sh`, and synchronize the verified package to the local add-on runtime. Testing the Instant path requires an Anki fork build that includes the corresponding backend implementation.
